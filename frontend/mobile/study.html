Question

What are the conditions for bail under the Administration of Criminal Justice Act 2015?

touch_app Tap to reveal answer
Answer check_circle

Under S. 158–185 ACJA 2015, bail may be granted considering: (1) nature & gravity of the offence, (2) likelihood of fleeing, (3) character & antecedents of the accused, (4) strength of evidence against the suspect, (5) whether offence involves a firearm.

⚖️ Authority

Sections 158–185, Administration of Criminal Justice Act 2015
