Medium Criminal Litigation

Under the Administration of Criminal Justice Act 2015, what is the maximum period a suspect may be held in custody without being charged to court where a court of competent jurisdiction is not within 40km?

✅ Correct Answer: B — 48 Hours

Section 35(5) CFRN 1999 and Section 30 ACJA 2015 provide that where a court of competent jurisdiction is not within 40km, the suspect may be held for up to 48 hours before being charged.
